### Let's Talk Research

As an undergraduate, I tried projects in quite a few different fields. My work was fairly wide-ranging, and those experiences helped me figure out which questions I wanted to spend more time on. During my PhD, I've narrowed my focus to research that brings together wave energy, fluid mechanics, and AI. For example, how can we build our physical understanding of fluids into a model? And how can we tell whether its results make sense for real wave problems? I'm still working through these questions. If you do related research, I'd be glad to hear about your experience, the problems you've run into, or how you see these questions differently. I have a lot to learn and welcome any corrections.
